01 · Relationship layer

From a flat "ingredient" text field to a real graph

The source data stored ingredients as free text inside each product row — no structure, no reuse. The first modeling step splits that into a proper many-to-many relationship, then surfaces which ingredients most frequently co-occur in the same formula.
